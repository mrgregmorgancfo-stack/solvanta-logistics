Important Information

Understanding the scope of coordination and the details that must be confirmed helps avoid misunderstandings.

Quotes & Charges

Quotes and estimated costs depend on the information supplied and the rates and conditions confirmed by the relevant service provider. Additional charges may apply where disclosed in the agreed terms.

Transit & Delivery

Transit times, collection dates, delivery dates and availability are estimates until confirmed by the relevant provider. Delays may occur due to operational, weather, customs or other circumstances.

Tracking & Updates

Public tracking displays recorded shipment information available in our system. Unless expressly confirmed, it should not be interpreted as live GPS or a direct carrier tracking feed.

Before booking: Confirm the service provider, shipment scope, charges, insurance or liability arrangements, applicable customs requirements and any cancellation terms relevant to your shipment.
