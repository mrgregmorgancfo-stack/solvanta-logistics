Customer Information

Shipping, Refund & Cancellation Policies

We aim to make shipping arrangements clear and straightforward. Please review these policies before confirming a shipment. Your booking confirmation and any applicable laws also apply.

1. Payment Before Shipment

Full payment of the agreed shipment charges must be received and confirmed before the shipment is dispatched. Unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise, we do not dispatch shipments on a payment-later basis.

  • A quotation should be accepted and the applicable charges confirmed before the booking proceeds.
  • Dispatch is subject to payment confirmation and the arrangements agreed with the relevant logistics partner.
  • Additional charges, such as customs duties, taxes, inspections, storage or handling fees, will be explained where known and allocated according to the booking terms.
  • Payment does not guarantee a specific delivery date unless that commitment has been expressly confirmed in writing.

2. Customer Cancellation

Customers should submit cancellation requests in writing as soon as possible using the contact details on this website. Cancellation options depend on the stage of the shipment.

  • Before a booking is confirmed or costs are committed, payments will be reviewed for a refund, subject to applicable law and the circumstances.
  • After confirmation, reasonable costs already incurred or third-party charges that cannot be recovered may be deducted where permitted by law and the agreed terms.
  • Once goods have been collected, dispatched or handed over to a carrier, cancellation may not be possible.
  • Return, redirection, storage, collection and handling charges may apply if the customer requests a change after arrangements have begun.
  • We will explain known charges and available options before proceeding where reasonably possible.

3. Refund Policy

Refund requests are assessed individually, taking account of the service booked, shipment stage, costs incurred, applicable partner terms and legal consumer rights.

  • Approved refunds will normally be returned through the original payment method where practicable.
  • We aim to process approved refunds within 5–10 business days. Banks and payment providers may take additional time to make the funds available.
  • Charges for services already performed and third-party costs that cannot be recovered may be excluded where lawful and consistent with the agreed terms.
  • If the agreed service cannot be provided, we will review the circumstances and explain any available refund, remedy or alternative arrangement under the booking terms and applicable law.
  • Nothing in this policy removes rights to a refund or other remedy that cannot legally be excluded.

4. Delivery Times and Delays

Transit times are estimates unless a specific delivery commitment has been confirmed in writing. Delays may arise from customs inspections, adverse weather, port congestion, carrier schedules, security checks or other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

We aim to communicate material updates received from relevant logistics partners. A delay does not automatically qualify for a refund; requests will be considered under the agreed service terms and applicable law.

5. Fragile, Valuable and Special-Care Goods

Customers are strongly advised to arrange suitable transit insurance for fragile, valuable, delicate or easily damaged goods before shipment.

  • Examples include glassware, artwork, antiques, musical instruments, electronics and delicate equipment.
  • Customers should accurately declare the nature and value of goods and use suitable protective packaging.
  • Insurance is not automatically included unless expressly confirmed in writing.
  • Insurance cover, exclusions, excesses, limits and claims requirements depend on the insurer and policy. Customers should review the policy carefully.
  • If insurance is requested, obtain written confirmation of whether cover has been arranged, its insured value and its relevant terms before dispatch.

6. Damage, Loss and Claims

Customers should inspect delivered goods promptly and report visible damage, missing items or suspected loss as soon as possible.

  • Contact us with the shipment reference, photographs, a description of the issue and supporting documents.
  • Keep the original packaging and damaged items where possible, as they may be required for an assessment.
  • Carrier and insurer claim deadlines may be short. Follow the applicable instructions and submit evidence within the required time.
  • Claims depend on the relevant contract, evidence, insurance terms where applicable and governing law.
  • Nothing in this policy excludes liability or rights that cannot legally be excluded.

7. Customs, Duties and Restricted Goods

International shipments may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, inspections, permits and destination-country requirements. Responsibility for these charges will be determined by the quotation and booking terms.

Customers must provide accurate shipment descriptions, declared values and required documents. Prohibited or restricted goods must not be tendered without the necessary authorisation. Delays, rejection, storage or return costs may arise if legal or documentation requirements are not met.

8. Logistics Partners and Service Scope

Solvanta Logistics coordinates shipments through logistics partners. Transport providers, routes, handling arrangements and available services depend on the shipment and partner confirmation.

The quotation and booking confirmation should identify the agreed service and relevant terms. Responsibility for transport, loss, damage and delays depends on the role of each provider, the applicable contract and governing law.

9. Changes to a Shipment

Requests to change the delivery address, destination, shipment contents or service after booking should be made in writing as soon as possible.

Changes are subject to operational feasibility and partner approval. Additional transport, storage, customs or administration charges may apply. We will communicate known charges before proceeding where reasonably possible.

10. Policy Questions and Disputes

If you have a question about a quotation, cancellation, refund or shipment, please contact us with your booking reference and a clear explanation of your concern.

We will review the available information and aim to resolve concerns fairly. These policies are subject to applicable laws and do not override mandatory consumer protections or other rights that cannot lawfully be waived.
