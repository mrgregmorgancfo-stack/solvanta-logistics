Our Commercial Business

Connecting Suppliers, Products & Business Customers

Solvanta Logistics is developing its commercial operations to support product sourcing, wholesale purchasing, supply and distribution across selected commercial sectors. What We Do

Our Commercial Activities

Our commercial activities are structured to support product enquiries, supplier relationships and business purchasing requirements.

Product Sourcing

Identifying suitable manufacturers, wholesalers and suppliers for products based on customer requirements, specifications and target markets.

Wholesale Procurement

Coordinating bulk purchasing enquiries, requesting quotations, reviewing supplier terms and supporting commercial procurement.

Product Supply

Supporting business customers with product availability enquiries, purchasing requirements and suitable supply arrangements.

Distribution

Developing distribution opportunities and coordinating the movement of products from suppliers to designated business markets.

Supplier Partnerships

Establishing commercial relationships with manufacturers, wholesalers and distributors interested in potential supply arrangements.

Supply Chain Coordination

Supporting the coordination of procurement, storage, freight and distribution requirements associated with commercial orders.
