Who We Are

About Solvanta Logistics

Solvanta Logistics is an international logistics and commercial supply company focused on connecting businesses with products, suppliers and coordinated logistics solutions.


Our business operates across two complementary areas: logistics operations and commercial operations. Through these activities, we support transportation, storage, procurement, product supply and distribution requirements.


We aim to establish productive relationships with manufacturers, wholesalers, logistics providers, distribution partners and business customers. Our approach is built around effective coordination, clear communication and practical commercial solutions.

Our Business

Two Complementary Areas of Operation

We bring together logistics capabilities and commercial opportunities to support the movement and supply of products across business markets.

Logistics Operations

Our logistics operations focus on coordinating freight transportation, warehousing, storage, specialist handling, road transport and last-mile delivery through suitable logistics arrangements and service providers.
